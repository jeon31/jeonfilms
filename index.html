About

Who I am

Every frame is a chance to say something true.

I'm Jivan, the filmmaker behind Jeon Films, originally from Nepal and now based in Utah, United States. With over five years of hands-on experience in visual production, I bring craft, intention and a cross-cultural perspective to every project I take on.

I work across video editing, cinematography, animation and visual effects. From short films and commercials to branded content and client productions, I always lead with the story and let the craft follow.

Whether shaping raw footage or starting from nothing, I give it everything I have.
